# High-win-rate research screen v4

UNPROVEN / research only. Freeze this specification before the first screen. No live orders, no martingale, no averaging, no changes to v1/v2/v3 or live paper risk controls. Objective: look for an observed 80% win rate AND positive modeled expectancy, not manufacture that percentage.

Bounded screen: three fixed signal families × two minimum NET reward/risk gates (0.5 and 1.0) × two constant spreads ($6.40 and $12.80 per BTC). Twelve candidate/scenario combinations, no parameter optimization after results. The 0.5R/1R gates are explicit new research experiments; they do not relax the approved v1 2R rule. Smaller targets require more frequent wins and can hide large-loss risk.

Same frozen Exness bid OHLC snapshot as previous tests. Primary window: seven days ending at the existing common M1/M5 end. Earlier comparison window: the immediately preceding three days, separately reset and reported. Both are historical/exploratory; earlier-window results are not an untouched prospective validation and no best candidate is selected automatically.

All signals use only completed contiguous candles. Indicators for signal candle use PRIOR candles, excluding that candle. M1 Bollinger/ATR/range warmup needs 21 completed prior bars. M5 context needs 40 completed contiguous prior bars (rolling60 EMA20). Reject a context candle whose close lies after signal candle OPEN. After a data gap, cancel pending signal and require full contiguous indicator warmup again. Never use current/forming candles.

Signal families:
1. BB_REENTRY: prior20 M1 close mean +/-2 population standard deviations. Long: previous close <= prior lower band, current close > lower band, bullish body; short mirrored. M5 context must be flat: absolute EMA20 change across last5 M5 bars <=0.25 of M5 ATR14. Target fixed prior20 mean. Stop signal low/high plus0.25 M1 ATR14.
2. FAILED_RANGE_BREAK: prior20 M1 high/low boundaries. Long signal wick below prior low, bullish close back above low; short above prior high and bearish close back below high. Same flat-M5 filter. Target fixed range midpoint. Stop signal extreme plus0.25 M1 ATR14.
3. EMA_TREND_RECLAIM: M5 close above rising EMA20 for longs, below falling for shorts. M1 prior EMA9 above EMA21 for longs, mirrored short. Signal candle touches EMA9, closes back above/below it with directional body, and previous M1 close was on the pullback side of prior EMA9. Stop signal extreme plus0.25 M1 ATR14. Target fixed one prior M1 ATR from signal close in trade direction.

Entry only next contiguous M1 open after signal; freeze stop/target at signal. USD5 adverse slippage/BTC/fill, USD0 modeled commission, bid OHLC and constant modeled ask for short exits; contract1, tick.01, min/step.01 lots, max200. Round stop outward and target inward. Gate on potential net reward/risk after slippage/spread; skip if invalid. Initial1000, risk min(5,.5%equity), one position, 30min time exit, stop-first ambiguous bars and adverse opening gaps. Stop trigger checked before target when order unknowable. Time exit at opening quote. Never reverse on exit bar. An open position crossing missing M1 data is uncertain and excluded; stop run rather than invent exit. Open end positions excluded. Historical margin, receipt latency and news review unverified.

Risk: pause remainder of each separately initialized research window after three consecutive losses or cumulative negative closed P/L20. No automatic resume. Count risk-suppressed signals separately; small samples/paused windows cannot prove true win rate. This does not change live paper controls.

Report all results, not just best: N, wins/losses, observed win fraction, Wilson95% interval (independence approximation; clustered trades can widen actual uncertainty), average win/loss, profit factor, modeled net P/L/costs, closed-equity drawdown, ambiguous/gap/open trades and risk pauses. A flag for further research requires >=100 closed trades, observed win>=80%, positive net P/L, and no excluded uncertain/open positions in BOTH windows and BOTH cost scenarios for the same fixed signal/gate. Even passing that flag is not a guarantee of future 80% wins. If no candidate meets it, report none. No post-result grid expansion or favorable redefinition of a win (win strictly net P/L>0; breakeven is not win).
